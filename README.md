## About Me

<img src="assets/gif/cat-hardcore.gif" align="right" width="150" alt="Cat hacking on a laptop" />

I started on the backend and learned just enough frontend to demo systems end to end — the [smartwatch platform](https://github.com/dungtq2k5/smartwatch-e-commerce) from my second year was that first serious full-stack build (in my opinion), before "vibe coding" had a name.

A year of building with AI changed where the hard part sits. Writing code is no longer the bottleneck; what matters now is understanding **how systems behave** — what breaks when one piece changes, whether an AI suggestion actually fits the architecture, whether the result satisfies the business logic, and which mechanism (caching, throttling, an event bus) the problem genuinely needs. That shift pulled me past backend into infrastructure and DevOps.

I build **microservices** — TypeScript across the services, Python where retrieval and models demand it — and I work solo by preference. I would rather ship one ambitious system under a tight deadline than five safe ones that teach nothing.

### SynapseDesk — Enterprise AI Helpdesk

#### Jul 2026 – Present &nbsp;·&nbsp; Personal&Course project &nbsp;·&nbsp; [*Live*](https://synapsedeskapp.com)

**Description.** Multi-tenant AI helpdesk: AI answers from a company's own documents with citations, and hands off to a human agent when it can't.

**Architecture & tech**

- 7 services over **gRPC**: a **Spring Boot** gateway (ported from NestJS, held to parity by a 2,500-row contract suite), 5 **NestJS** services and a **Python** RAG service; a database per service.
- **NATS JetStream** events, **Redis** cache and queues, hybrid search in **Qdrant** with reranking, **Gemini** for answers; **Next.js** client.

**Features** — cited AI chat, email-to-ticket, agent workspace with an AI co-pilot, knowledge-base quality flags, RBAC + 2FA, analytics, **Stripe** plans with metered AI usage.

**Quality & delivery** — tests in TS, Java and Python plus 640+ Playwright rows; CI on GitHub Actions with a SonarCloud gate; **52 ADRs**; self-hosted live demo via Docker and Cloudflare Tunnel.

### BrewLite — Cashless Café Ordering

#### Sep 2026 – Present &nbsp;·&nbsp; Course project

**Description.** Order ahead and pay without cash at a coffee shop, then collect at the counter when your number is called — an installable **PWA** in English and Vietnamese.

**Architecture & tech**

- A **NestJS** gateway and 4 services (identity, catalog, ordering, payment) over **gRPC**; one **PostgreSQL** server with a database per service, **Prisma**, **Redis**.
- **NATS JetStream** events via a transactional outbox, self-healing after a broker reset; a **Next.js 16** BFF behind Caddy streams order status over SSE; **Stripe** embedded Checkout plus a fake provider with identical webhook events.

**Features** — guest browsing, cart, server-priced checkout with promo codes and loyalty points, live order tracking, a staff order board with undo, an admin back office (menu, promotions, users, sales).

**Quality & delivery** — 490+ unit, 280+ integration and 130 Playwright tests, incl. concurrency proofs that stock never oversells and a retry never double-charges; a one-command smoke test; CI on GitHub Actions with a SonarCloud gate; **35 ADRs**.

### SmartDoc AI — Local, Private Document Intelligence

#### Mar 2026 – May 2026 &nbsp;·&nbsp; Course project

**Description.** A NotebookLM-style assistant that runs entirely on your own machine: upload PDFs and Word documents, ask in multi languages (optimized for English & Vietnamese), and get answers grounded in those documents with source citations — nothing leaves the laptop.

**Architecture & tech**

- A **Streamlit** UI over a dual-pipeline RAG core built with **LangChain**: **Self-RAG** (multi-hop retrieval, groundedness/relevance/utility quality gates, a repair agent that retries failed searches) and **Co-RAG** (Generator↔Reviewer loop), both run on every query and shown side by side.
- Hybrid retrieval with **FAISS** embeddings (multilingual Sentence Transformers) plus **BM25**, cross-encoder re-ranking and a configurable weight; local inference with **Ollama** (Qwen2.5 7B); **SQLite** for notebooks, chat history and notes.

**Features** — Markdown-aware PDF/DOCX ingestion, automatic chunk citations, per-notebook retrieval and prompt settings, saved study notes, a hardware-aware document hub (RAM/VRAM).

**Quality & delivery** — versioned releases with a changelog, 8 Mermaid architecture diagrams, a centralised config reference and setup/hardware guidance for 8 GB machines; MIT licensed.
